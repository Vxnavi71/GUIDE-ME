# Changelog

All notable changes to **GUIDE ME - FINAL** are documented in this file.

## [1.0.0] - 2026-09-06
### Added
- **Complete Full-Stack Enterprise Prototype**: React 18 + TypeScript frontend and FastAPI + SQLAlchemy 2.0 backend.
- **Eco-Friendly Calm UI**: Sustainable sage and earth palette with dark/light themes and reduced motion.
- **Privacy-First Facial Analysis**: Real `navigator.mediaDevices.getUserMedia()` integration with in-memory frame processing.
- **Big Five (OCEAN) Psychometrics**: 50-item IPIP questionnaire, scoring algorithms, and career alignment mapping.
- **NLP Reflection Engine**: Continuous sentiment score, dominant emotion tagging, cognitive distortion detection, and constructive re-framing.
- **Multimodal Stress & Distress Monitor**: Personal rolling baseline comparison (z-score) and multi-signal evidence fusion.
- **AI Companion**: Clinical heuristic with transparent memory management, safety crisis guardrails, and typing rhythm dynamics.
- **Wearable Hub & Digital Wellbeing**: Modular provider architecture (Mock, Apple HealthKit, Fitbit, Google Health Connect) and screen exposure analytics.
- **Safety & Privacy Centers**: Configurable global emergency hotlines, trusted contacts, full JSON data export, and complete account deletion.
- **Git Bash Automation Scripts**: `setup.sh`, `start_backend.sh`, `start_frontend.sh`, `start_all.sh`, `test.sh`, `seed.sh`.
