# GUIDE ME - Research & Psychometric Foundations

## 1. The Big Five (OCEAN) Personality Model

The Five-Factor Model represents the preeminent evidence-based consensus in personality psychology:
1. **Openness to Experience**: Intellectual curiosity, artistic sensitivity, and preference for variety.
2. **Conscientiousness**: Self-discipline, systematic organization, and goal-directed persistence.
3. **Extraversion**: Assertiveness, sociability, positive emotionality, and energetic enthusiasm.
4. **Agreeableness**: Empathy, altruism, cooperativeness, and trust.
5. **Neuroticism (Emotional Stability Inverted)**: Sensitivity to negative stimuli, threat monitoring, and emotional reactivity.

*Implementation Note:* GUIDE ME implements the validated International Personality Item Pool (IPIP) 50-item questionnaire with standard reverse-keyed scoring.

## 2. Autonomic Tone & Heart Rate Variability (HRV)

Heart rate variability (HRV), specifically root-mean-square of successive differences (RMSSD), serves as a non-invasive proxy for parasympathetic nervous system activity via the vagus nerve. Higher HRV generally correlates with psychological flexibility and autonomic resilience, whereas depressed HRV indicates sustained sympathetic activation.

## 3. Cognitive Distortions in NLP

Derived from Cognitive Behavioral Therapy (Beck, 1979; Burns, 1980), cognitive distortions represent systematic biases in informational processing:
- **All-or-Nothing Thinking**: Rigid dichotomy without recognizing spectrums.
- **Catastrophizing**: Magnifying the likelihood of catastrophic outcomes.
- **Overgeneralization**: Drawing global conclusions from single negative events.
- **Emotional Reasoning**: Assuming subjective emotional reactions constitute objective empirical facts.

*GUIDE ME* detects these patterns in journal reflections to offer compassionate, objective re-framing prompts rather than diagnostic labels.

## 4. Responsible AI & Clinical Boundaries

- Emotional inference from text, typing, or facial proxies is fundamentally **probabilistic**.
- Facial expressions vary widely across cultures and do not prove internal affective states.
- The system is explicitly an educational self-awareness companion and **not a diagnostic medical device**.
